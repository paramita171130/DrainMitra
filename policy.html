TRANSPARENCY & RESPONSIBLE USE

Policies, privacy & terms

Plain-language terms for the DrainMitra concept dashboard. This page describes the current demonstration, not a fully launched monitoring service.

Last updated: 21 October 2026
Important demo notice: Account access uses verified email codes and profile details are stored in the app database. The dashboard is still a prototype: readings and activity figures are simulated and are not connected to live drains or sensors.

1. Demo policy

DrainMitra is a student innovation concept intended to demonstrate a possible drain-monitoring interface. It is not connected to live sensors, municipal systems, emergency services, or a verified maintenance team.

  • All dashboard readings, alerts, waste totals, and maintenance records shown by default are illustrative demo data.
  • Do not use this interface to make safety-critical decisions, dispatch workers, or assess flood risk.
  • A physical drain basket or sensor installation must be professionally assessed for hydraulic capacity, bypass behavior, electrical/weather protection, safe access, and maintenance before any real-world use.
  • Account sign-in is handled by the hosting platform using emailed one-time codes. Your profile is saved server-side and can be retrieved when you sign in again with the same verified email.
  • We may change or reset demo functionality while developing the concept.

2. Terms & conditions

By using the DrainMitra demo, you agree to use it only for lawful evaluation, learning, feedback, and presentation purposes.

Prototype and availability

The service is provided as-is for demonstration, without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability, data accuracy, or suitability for a particular purpose. Interface elements may be incomplete and sample data may not represent actual locations or conditions.

No emergency or engineering advice

DrainMitra is not an emergency-response service and does not replace qualified drainage engineers, site inspections, official advisories, or routine cleaning. Never rely on simulated alerts to protect life, property, or infrastructure.

Acceptable use

Do not attempt to disrupt the demo, upload unlawful content, impersonate another person, or submit sensitive data. You are responsible for ensuring that any information you enter is appropriate for a public-facing prototype.

Intellectual property and changes

The DrainMitra name, concept, and interface are part of a developing project. Permission is required before representing the prototype as an operational municipal service or claiming verified impact.

Limitations

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the prototype team is not responsible for decisions made based on illustrative data or for losses arising from reliance on an unvalidated concept. Nothing here limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.

3. Privacy notice

We aim to collect as little information as possible in this prototype. Please read this section before entering details.

Information requested

Sign-up asks for your full name and email address, with an optional organization. The hosting platform verifies access using a one-time code sent to your email; this app does not ask you to create or store a password.

Where account details are stored

Your verified account is managed by the hosting platform, and your DrainMitra profile (name, email, organization, and timestamps) is stored in this project's database and associated with your account. Do not enter sensitive personal data, government IDs, financial details, or information about other people.

Sharing and tracking

The hosting provider processes account and technical data needed to authenticate users and deliver the site. Externally hosted fonts may make requests to their provider. The dashboard's sensor values are simulated and are not personal account data.

Retention and your choices

You can sign out of the app. Profile changes and account deletion are not yet available from the dashboard; do not use the prototype for information requiring special confidentiality or a guaranteed deletion workflow.

Future production version

Before a real public launch, DrainMitra would need properly configured authentication, server-side storage, access controls, retention/deletion procedures, security testing, and a reviewed privacy notice that identifies the responsible organization and contact details. This page is not a substitute for legal review.

4. Questions and feedback

This page accompanies a student prototype. No official support contact or data-controller address has been established for the demo yet. Please do not submit confidential information through this interface.
